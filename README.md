# EX No.4.1. CUT SECTION OF SOLIDS
## DATE: 11/11/2026

## AIM
To create the solid model from given drawing and section the solid model

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3.16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:
1.	Open new file and then select sketch icon.
2.	Select Sketch and Create Sketch and pick the Top (XY) plane. Draw two circles of  radius 15mm centred 70 mm apart, then a Ø50 circle in the middle. Draw tangent lines joining the radius 15mm circles to the Ø50 circle see from the top view. Trim the extra arcs. Click Finish Sketch.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825274-c6a8d3ee-fb19-4641-b915-8d2e000c40a8.png)

3.	Select Extrude, click the base shape and type 10 mm. Press enter.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825282-0d3de4b8-b6d9-446a-ae2a-461955057399.png)

4.	Create a new sketch on the top face of the base. Draw a Ø50 circle at the centre. Click Finish Sketch, then Extrude it 18 mm

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825301-194365b4-69d2-42ad-8545-a68d3ca1f442.png)

5.	Create a new sketch on the top face of the cylinder. Draw a Ø30 circle at the centre, then Finish Sketch. Use Extrude, choose Cut, and enter 6 mm. Next, sketch a Ø20 circle on the bottom of that recess and Finish Sketch, and then Extrude that Cut through all.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825314-b90f6aba-da26-4f0d-8d61-a03e8ad0d765.png)

7.Create a sketch on the top face of the base plate. Draw a Ø12 circle at the centre of each R15 end. Finish Sketch, then Extrude and then Create a sketch on the Front plane. Draw a Ø6 circle at a height of 12 mm from the top of the cylinder, in line with the centre. Finish Sketch, then Extrude > Cut until it meets the centre hole.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825328-205fac21-1322-4d67-894b-6fd92e454e26.png)

8.Use revolve option to convert 2D to 3D.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825337-455049de-f053-48b8-9dd0-ebeb189c0dde.png)
